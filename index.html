How I work

A product loop,
in practice.

The same four steps run through everything I've shipped at Triton.

01 · Discover
Understand the buyer

Mapped how procurement engineers search, by grade designation rather than internal product codes, and structured the site around those queries.

02 · Prioritise
Decide what to build first

Sequenced 80+ pages by buyer intent and expected impact, starting with grade and comparison pages closest to a purchase decision.

03 · Ship
Build it end to end

Designed, wrote, built and launched the site, plus a weight calculator that answered a task buyers already did before requesting a quote.

04 · Measure
Read the data, then adjust

GA4 events and Search Console were live before launch. When the April 2026 pivot cut traffic, I tracked the recovery and documented the cause.

Problem: a B2B steel exporter selling to 187 countries, with no digital presence. Insight: buyers search by grade designation (S355JR, S355J2, S355MC), not product code. Action: built 80+ pages around those queries, wrote all content, and set up GA4 and Search Console before launch. Trade-off: in April 2026 I moved 50+ pages to India-specific targeting to match the company's shifting buyer base. Traffic fell about 61% while Google re-evaluated the site; I tracked it in Search Console and it is now recovering.
The site now earns sessions from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Copilot because each page is structured to give a clear, citable answer.
